Cookies

This site sets no non-essential cookies at all. The consent machinery exists anyway, and this page explains why.

What is stored today

One entry in your browser's local storage, named elysium.consent, recording the choice you made and when you made it. Nothing else — no analytics, no advertising, no third-party script of any kind.

That entry is not sent anywhere. It stays in your browser so the banner does not ask again on your next visit.

Why there is a banner with nothing to consent to

The machinery has to exist before the first analytics tag does, or that tag fires before anybody is asked. Consent must be obtained before a non-essential cookie is set, not shortly after.

The categories

  • Necessary — sign-in and security. Always on, and shown disabled rather than offered as a choice nobody would honour.
  • Analytics — how the site is used. Nothing in this category is in use today.
  • Marketing — advertising measurement. Nothing in this category is in use today.

Changing or withdrawing your choice

The Cookie settings link in the footer of every page reopens the panel with your current choices loaded. Withdrawing is as easy as giving, which is a requirement rather than a courtesy.

When this page changes

Adding a category invalidates consent given under the old set, so the banner asks again rather than treating an old answer as covering something nobody saw. This page will be updated in the same change that adds the tag.
