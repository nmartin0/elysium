Privacy

This site collects no personal data. The short version is the whole version.

Drafted in-house, not reviewed by a lawyer. This is adequate for a pre-customer site that collects almost nothing. Before Elysium has customers, paying users, or any form that accepts personal data, it needs replacing by someone qualified — particularly the sections on legal basis, international transfers and liability.

Last updated: October 2026.

What this covers

This notice covers elysium.example, the public website. It does not cover an Elysium deployment running inside your own organisation: that instance is operated by you, holds your data, and we have no access to it. See the trust centre for what the software does with data.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixel, no embedded third-party script, and no form on this site that accepts personal data. No account can be created here.

The only thing stored on your device is one entry in your browser's local storage, elysium.consent, recording your cookie choice and when you made it. It never leaves your browser and is not readable by us. The cookie policy explains it in full.

Server logs

Whoever hosts this site will keep ordinary web-server logs — IP address, time, page requested, user agent — for operational and security purposes. Those records are not combined with anything else, not used to build a profile, and not shared. The legal basis is legitimate interest in running a secure service.

Who we share it with

Nobody. There are no subprocessors for this website beyond the hosting provider serving the files. If that changes, this page will say so, with a date, before it changes.

How long we keep it

Server logs are retained for no longer than operational need, which for a static site is weeks rather than years. Your cookie choice stays in your browser until you clear it or change it.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you have the right to access, correct, delete and port your personal data, to object to processing, and to complain to a supervisory authority. In practice we hold almost nothing to which those rights could attach — but if you believe we hold something about you, ask, and you will get a straight answer.

Children

This site is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

Material changes will be dated here. A change that starts collecting something new will appear before the collection does, not after.

Contact

Privacy questions and requests: privacy@elysium.example. Security reports go to the address in security.txt instead.
